# Quick

Read [execution](../../references/execution.md), [runtime](../../references/runtime.md), and the [rubric](../../references/rubric.md). Before product edits, start or resume the authorized runtime; reconstruct it only when its record is unavailable, from the absolute project, run id, literal Engram topic and actual approval reference. Preserve missing indispensable identity or authority as the precise pending item. The user's clear request is authorization for the stated small change; do not insert a plan-approval ceremony. Preserve unrelated work.

Use [shared Engram memory](../../references/engram.md) for established preferences, relevant prior context and the final or paused summary. Preserve existing topics on resume; quick creates no artificial plan/index entries or operational Markdown.

Identify the expected behavior, touched surface, existing primitives and applicable verification commands. Represent the task as one block, including `rubric`, `conformance` and its actual behavioral criteria. Reproduce a meaningful failing case when the change needs one; do not invent tests for a trivial reversible edit.

For a new specification, record the approved child coordination and any approved actual review fallback. Existing records retain their compatible accounting rather than receiving retroactive configuration data.

Use the task's actual autonomy and limits. New approved AUTO uses the policy in [runtime](../../references/runtime.md); an existing finite or absent policy is preserved until explicit adoption. For a correction pause or authorized adoption, follow [correction recovery](../../references/correction-recovery.md) without adding a routine budget question to already covered work.

If discovery exposes substantial independent surfaces or unresolved architecture, recommend Plan with the concrete reason. For an observed bug, recommend Debug's reproduction and diagnosis route. Reuse the established scope and authorization; a routing recommendation is not an automatic stop or a new approval for covered work.

The principal owns decisions, integration and closure. Keep tiny, already-understood changes local; delegate substantial independent implementation, investigation or corrections under [model selection and escalation](../../references/execution.md#model-selection-and-escalation). Execute relevant checks once through `oso-codex check`; fix actual failures, reuse current evidence and rerun affected checks after changes.

Request one independent final review under [model selection and escalation](../../references/execution.md#model-selection-and-escalation), covering the full assigned surface for behavior, rubric and conformance, plus security/design criteria when relevant. It consumes current recorded results and batches supported findings. Apply related fixes together and have the same reviewer confirm affected contracts. A different report format is not a failed task.

Checkpoint and close only with all required criteria satisfied. Report the result and verification, including any real limitation. Do not add a second equivalent debt sweep after this combined review.
